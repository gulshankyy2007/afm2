Whole-Product Adaptive Product Architecture

AFM#2 rejects collapsing responsiveness. Desktop preserves its 8–10 column tabular ledgers, continuous multi-card scorecards, and high-density sorting power. Mobile receives a thumb-driven sanctuary with 2-column Pinterest discovery, 52px SoundCloud compact rows, and 3-step qualification wizards. Both share identical business rules, 8 atmospheric themes, and 5 scoring criteria.
